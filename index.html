Building AECHO

An AI infrastructure operating system — one control plane to deploy, validate, operate, and optimize infrastructure across cloud, Kubernetes, GPUs, and MLOps.

The problem I kept running into: every layer of AI infrastructure — cloud, Kubernetes, GPUs, observability, cost — is its own tool with its own mental model, and the failures live in the gaps between them. AECHO is my attempt at a shared understanding layer: it reasons across the dependency graph instead of showing another disconnected dashboard.

Early-stage, in active development.
